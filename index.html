INDIAN AGRICULTURE DIRECTORY

Crops Grown in India

Explore growing conditions, season classifications, soil requirements, and cultivation tips for 28+ major Indian crops including plantation & commercial crops.

🌾 Kharif • Cereal

Rice (Paddy)

Hot & Humid (20°C–37°C)

Soil: Clayey loams retaining standing water.

Tip: Maintain 2–5 cm standing water depth in early transplanting.

🌾 Rabi • Cereal

Wheat (Gehun)

Cool & Moist Winter (10°C–15°C)

Soil: Well-drained fertile loams and clayey loams.

Tip: Requires bright sunshine at the time of ripening and a frost-free period.

🌽 Kharif • Cereal

Maize (Makka)

Warm Climate (21°C–27°C)

Soil: Old alluvial soil with good drainage.

Tip: Highly sensitive to waterlogging; ensure proper field drainage.

🌾 Dryland • Cereal

Millets (Jowar/Bajra)

Hot & Dry (25°C–35°C)

Soil: Sandy, shallow, or black soils with low moisture.

Tip: Drought resistant; excellent for arid regions with poor rainfall.

🌱 Rabi • Pulse

Gram (Chana)

Cool Climate (20°C–25°C)

Soil: Light loamy or heavy clay soils.

Tip: Nipping (plucking top shoots) encourages branching and higher yield.

🍲 Kharif • Pulse

Pigeon Pea (Tur)

Warm & Moist (26°C–30°C)

Soil: Well-drained loamy soils.

Tip: Often grown as a mixed crop with cereals to fix soil nitrogen.

🥜 Kharif • Oilseed

Groundnut (Mungfali)

Warm Climate (20°C–30°C)

Soil: Sandy loams and well-drained soils.

Tip: Requires a dry period during ripening for easier harvesting.

🌼 Rabi • Oilseed

Mustard (Sarson)

Cool Climate (10°C–25°C)

Soil: Light loamy soils to heavy clays.

Tip: Susceptible to aphids; timely spraying of natural pesticides is crucial.

🍵 Plantation • Commercial

Tea (Chai)

Tropical & Subtropical (20°C–30°C)

Soil: Deep friable acidic soil (pH 4.5–5.5).

Tip: Requires heavy rainfall evenly distributed without water stagnation.

☕ Plantation • Commercial

Coffee

Hot & Humid Hill Slopes (15°C–28°C)

Soil: Deep, well-drained loamy soil.

Tip: Grown under two-tier shade trees to protect bushes from direct intense sunlight.

🎋 Perennial • Commercial

Sugarcane (Ganna)

Hot & Humid (10–14 Months)

Soil: Deep fertile alluvial loams.

Tip: High irrigation need; practice trash mulching for moisture retention.

☁️ Kharif • Commercial

Cotton (Kapas)

Warm (210 Frost-Free Days)

Soil: Deep black cotton soil (Regur).

Tip: Inspect regularly for pink bollworm infestations.

🥔 Tuber • Horticulture

Potato (Aloo)

Cool Temperate (15°C–20°C)

Soil: Loose, well-aerated loamy and sandy soils.

Tip: Earth up the base of the plant to prevent tubers from turning green from sunlight.

🧅 Bulb • Horticulture

Onion (Pyaz)

Mild Cool to Warm (13°C–30°C)

Soil: Deep, friable loam and alluvial soils.

Tip: Withhold irrigation a week before harvesting to improve storage life.
