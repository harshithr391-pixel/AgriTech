🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make farming knowledge simplified and responsive on any device. From low-cost mobile smartphones to high-resolution desktop displays, AgriTech delivers crop care tips, diagnostic demos, and weather updates.

📋 Core Objectives

  • ✓ Progressive Web App with mobile Touch & Install support.
  • ✓ Searchable crop care and climate recommendations.
  • ✓ Plant leaf health image upload and AI diagnostics.
  • ✓ Live searchable weather lookup utilizing Open-Meteo REST APIs.
