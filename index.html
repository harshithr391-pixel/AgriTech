ABOUT AGRITECH

Helping farmers grow a better future.

AgriTech combines accessible design with practical agricultural intelligence.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make precision agriculture simple, fast, and accessible on any device in every farmer's native language. AgriTech unites crop cultivation guides, AI leaf diagnostics, live weather updates, daily Mandi prices, machinery sharing, NPK calculators, Google Maps soil mapping, direct B2B buyers, and voice assistance.

📋 Platform Capabilities

  • ✓ Full-site native translation & hands-free voice assistant in Kannada, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, and English.
  • ✓ Searchable All-India crop directory synced with daily Agmarknet prices.
  • ✓ Camera & gallery plant leaf scanning, NPK dosage calculator, and interactive Google Maps Soil Health Card mapping.
  • ✓ Peer-to-peer machinery rental marketplace and direct B2B buyer lead board.
